Dot eBay integration privacy

This private, one-time pilot uses your eBay sign-in and reads your saved searches and the first page of your watched listings to confirm access. Your eBay password is entered only on eBay. The pilot does not purchase, bid, send offers, change your lists, or use the data for marketing.

The pilot shows only counts and short status information to the browser that started the test. It does not persist your saved searches, watched listings, authorization code or user tokens. Expiring cookies bind the request to your browser and carry the count-only result; the result is cleared when displayed. The server keeps its eBay application credentials separately in restricted hosting configuration.

The application does not log request contents or API response contents. Like most online services, eBay and the hosting provider may process transient request and security metadata under their own policies. You can revoke application access in your eBay account settings. For questions, contact the project owner who provided your access code.

Optional ongoing monitoring

If the owner separately connects ongoing monitoring, eBay grants read access to the owner’s watchlist and saved searches. With this consent, the service stores an encrypted refresh credential, stable eBay identifiers for account deletion, and limited monitoring state. Vercel runs the service; the owner’s Supabase project stores the encrypted account record and a private, minimal set of alert details. The service does not collect eBay passwords, purchase or shipping information, or contact details from the eBay profile.

The service checks for changes to watched listings and new matches, and can report seller-offer events if eBay grants that capability. It does not bid, buy, message sellers or change eBay lists. Disconnect from the connection page to remove active stored data, or revoke the app in eBay account settings to stop authorized access. The service processes verified eBay account deletion notices.
